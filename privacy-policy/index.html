Privacy Policy

Riyadh Velvet Factory ("RVF", "we") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through www.rvf.sa and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact form or by email, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number and message. We do not sell this information.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry and to provide information about our products and services.

Third-party services

Our contact form is delivered by a third-party form service, our contact page shows an embedded Google map. These services may process technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.
